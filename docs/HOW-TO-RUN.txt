EKrecorder - capture test (Step 1 spike)
=========================================

This is an engineering test build, not the finished recorder. It records nothing to video.
It checks that EKrecorder can capture a whole monitor without the yellow border, and that the
blue corner triangle and the Identify numbers stay OUT of the captured picture.

1. Double-click EKrecorder.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (This test build is not signed.)

2. Pick Monitor 1 or Monitor 2. EKrecorder numbers monitors from left to right.
   Not sure which is which? Click "Identify": each screen shows its number for 3 seconds.

3. Click "Run 10-second test" and watch the chosen monitor:
   - After 1 second a tiny blue triangle appears in its bottom-right corner.
   - Next to it, a small square blinks pink and green. That is a test marker that is
     deliberately NOT hidden from the capture, so the test can prove it sees that corner.
   - From 4.5 to 6.5 seconds every monitor shows its big blue number.
   - Check whether a yellow border appears around the monitor.

4. Answer the two Yes/No questions.

5. A folder opens on your Desktop under "EKrecorder test results". Open report.txt.
   The first lines say PASS or FAIL. Paste report.txt into our chat.
   The corner-*.png pictures show the bottom-right corner of the captured frames, 8 times
   larger: the blue triangle must NOT be in them. Compare them with
   corner-REFERENCE-if-the-triangle-were-captured-x8.png, which shows what a failure looks like.
