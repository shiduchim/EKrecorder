EKrecorder - test build (Step 3: microphone and computer audio)
================================================================

This build records one whole monitor to an MP4 file, with the microphone and the
computer audio mixed into one sound track. Video: at most 1920x1080, 15 frames per
second, H.264. Sound: AAC, 48 kHz stereo, 128 kbps.

1. Double-click EKrecorder.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (This test build is not signed.)

2. Pick Monitor 1 or Monitor 2. EKrecorder numbers monitors from left to right.
   Not sure which is which? Click "Identify": each screen shows its number for 3 seconds.

3. Audio. The Windows defaults are right for most tests:
   Microphone:      "Windows default communications microphone"
   Computer audio:  "Windows default outputs" (normal and communications output)
   You can also pick one specific microphone or output to test it.
   Tick "Show audio meters" to see the levels before recording (this opens the
   devices; untick it, or minimize the window, and they close again).

4. Click "Start recording". The small blue triangle appears in the bottom-right corner
   of that monitor while it records. It is not in the video.

5. Use the PC, then click "Stop recording". The window shows, for the microphone
   and the computer audio, the level, the state (Running / Fallback / Lost /
   Retrying) and the device in use.

6. The finished video is saved in Desktop > EKrecordings.
   A report opens in Notepad. Copy its SUMMARY part and paste it into our chat.
   For audio tests, also copy the AUDIO EVENTS part.
   If the report has a FAILURE DETAILS part, paste the whole report instead.
   "Open last report" opens it again later. "Open recordings" opens the video folder.

Good audio tests (each one: the recording must go on and keep its sound):
   - A Zoom or Teams call of a few minutes, talking and listening.
   - Unplug the headset (or switch Bluetooth off) during the recording, wait a few
     seconds, plug it back in.
   - Change the default device in Windows sound settings during the recording.
   - Mute the microphone for more than 10 seconds: a warning must appear.

While recording, the file is kept in %LocalAppData%\EKrecorder\InProgress and moved to
Desktop\EKrecordings when you stop. Closing the window also stops and saves the recording.

"Capture test" still runs the Step 1 test (triangle and Identify numbers hidden from capture).
